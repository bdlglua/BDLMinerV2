# BDLMiner V2 2.5.8

Fix a false Bitaxe Gamma 601 overtemperature fault while the ASIC is powered
off, including during a normal thermal pause.

- The external ASIC diode is used only after core power and diode routing are
  initialized. While the ASIC is off, the board sensor supplies the cooldown
  temperature. A floating diode value such as 106 C no longer blocks startup
  or turns a thermal pause into a restart-required fault.
- Full fan cooling during startup and pauses remains active. The running ASIC's
  temperature, regulator, supply, current and stopped-fan interlocks remain
  active.

## Temperature and fan settings

With the default 65 C thermal limit, reaching that temperature pauses mining.
The ASIC stays off for at least two minutes and may resume only once the
temperature is at most 60 C. During the pause the board sensor is used because
the ASIC diode is unpowered. The fan stays at 100 percent.

During mining, temperature safety forces 100 percent fan at ASIC 60 C or
regulator 80 C, including in manual mode. A lower configured thermal limit
makes it intervene earlier. The requested manual speed remains saved. The
independent hard stops at ASIC 75 C and regulator 105 C remain active.

This fix addresses a false reading while stopped; it does not reduce measured
heat under load. If thermal pauses recur, check the cooling and the saved
frequency/voltage profile. Stock Gamma settings are 525 MHz, 1150 mV and manual
fan 100 percent. Higher frequency or voltage increases heat and power. Existing
settings are retained; the update does not silently reset an overclock.

## Updating and validation

Use the signed GitHub updater, or upload the matching
`bitaxe-gamma-601-firmware.bin` through the Gamma's local dashboard. Factory
images are for USB installation. Other board profiles are included as usual.

The Gamma driver regressions cover false off-state readings, full cooling,
the 65 C pause, reset and regulator shutdown, the minimum cooldown hold and
restart, real ASIC/regulator overtemperature, and fan failures. Native, browser
and tooling checks and a local Gamma cross-build pass. The release pipeline
builds all 12 board profiles, validates the images and signs them centrally.
The cooling sequence is tested with simulated I2C; no new physical cooling
measurement is claimed for this release.
