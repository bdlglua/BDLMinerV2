See your miners

Enter the payout address your miners use. It is the one in Settings → Pool on each board.

Anyone who knows this address can see these readings, as on your pool's page. Nothing here can change a miner. A private access key created before BDLMiner 2.5.4 also works in this field.

Waiting for your first miner

On each miner (BDLMiner 2.5.4 or newer), open Settings → Network → Private remote monitoring, enable it, then save and restart. The miner uses its payout address: nothing to copy. The first report arrives after about one minute.
